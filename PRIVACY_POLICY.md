# Movement Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [fill in date before publishing]_

This policy covers the Movement iOS app ("Movement", "the app").

## Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email address or phone number** and a **username**, used to sign you in.
- **Birthday, gender, fitness goals, and experience level**, which you enter
  during onboarding and which we use to personalize workout recommendations.
- **Workout completion history** (which workouts you did, when, and the
  sets/reps prescribed), used to show your progress and streaks.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the name and email address
those services share with us, subject to your choices in their sign-in flow.

## How we store your information

Your account is managed by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). Your
profile, settings, and workout history are stored in Firestore (also a
Google/Firebase service), tied to your account and readable only by you.

## How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate the app: authenticating you,
personalizing workout prescriptions, and showing your progress. We do not
sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for
their own purposes.

## Tracking

Movement does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and
does not use advertising identifiers.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your
device. These are generated on-device and are not sent from a server.

## Your choices

- You can edit your profile and settings at any time in the app.
- You can turn off reminders in Settings.
- You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from
  Settings → Delete Account. This immediately and irreversibly removes your
  account and stored data.
